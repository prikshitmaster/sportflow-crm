Privacy Policy

Khelit — last updated 31 July 2026

Khelit ("the App") is a sports academy management platform used by academy owners, staff/coaches, parents, and students to manage batches, attendance, payments, and communication. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's protected.

Who this applies to

Khelit is used by four types of accounts: academy owners, staff/coaches, parents, and students. Student and parent accounts are created and managed by the academy administering the app, not self-registered by children.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address or phone number, and login credentials, for owners, staff, parents, and students.
  • Contact details: phone numbers used for login (parent accounts) and communication.
  • Academy operational data: attendance records, batch and session assignments, session/drill notes, and performance tracking entered by coaches or staff.
  • Payment records: fee amounts, payment status, and transaction references for academy fee collection. Card and UPI payment processing itself is handled by our payment processor (Razorpay); we do not store full card numbers.
  • Photos: session or drill photos uploaded by coaches, where the academy chooses to use this feature.
  • Device and push notification tokens: used to deliver in-app notifications and reminders via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Camera access: used only for QR-code based attendance scanning; photos taken are not stored unless explicitly used as a session photo upload.

How we use this information

  • To operate core academy features: attendance, batch scheduling, fee tracking, and staff/parent/student communication.
  • To send notifications (e.g. payment reminders, batch announcements) via push notification and in-app alerts.
  • To authenticate accounts and enforce role-based access, so parents and students only see data relevant to them.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — push notification delivery on Android.
  • Razorpay — payment processing for online fee collection.

Data security

All data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to academy data is restricted by role-based permissions, so staff, parents, and students can only access data relevant to their own academy and role.

Data retention and deletion

We retain academy data for as long as the academy's account is active. To request deletion of your personal data, contact us at the email below — we will process deletion requests unless we're required to retain certain records (e.g. payment records) for legal or accounting purposes.

Children's data

Some student accounts belong to minors. This data is provided and managed by the academy and/or the student's parent or guardian, not collected directly from children through open self-registration.
